## A. Thermodynamics of computation and fundamental constants

**[landauer1961]** R. Landauer. *Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process*. IBM Journal of Research and Development 5(3), 183-191. 1961-07. doi:10.1147/rd.53.0183. <https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5389195>

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Logical irreversibility implies a minimum heat generation 'of the order of kT for each irreversible function' (abstract); origin of kT ln 2 per erased bit.
* Limits / supersession: Argument made plausible rather than proven in 1961; later experimental tests (berut2012, jun2014) and finite-time analyses (proesmans2020) support it.

**[swanson1972]** R. M. Swanson, J. D. Meindl. *Ion-Implanted Complementary MOS Transistors in Low-Voltage Circuits*. IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits SC-7(2), 146-153. 1972-04.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Weak-inversion analysis of the CMOS inverter: smallest functional supply ~8 kT/q (abstract).

**[bennett1973]** C. H. Bennett. *Logical Reversibility of Computation*. IBM Journal of Research and Development 17(6), 525-532. 1973-11. doi:10.1147/rd.176.0525.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Any computation can be embedded in a logically reversible one (compute, copy output, uncompute) at the cost of extra steps (~2x) and temporary storage; so kT ln2 per step is not a necessary cost of computation, only of erasure.

**[margolus1998]** N. Margolus, L. B. Levitin. *The maximum speed of dynamical evolution*. Physica D 120(1-2), 188-195. 1998. doi:10.1016/S0167-2789(98)00054-2. <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9710043>

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Minimum time to an orthogonal state t >= h/(4E) with E the mean energy above the ground state; at most ~3e33 operations per second per joule.

**[meindl2000]** J. D. Meindl, J. A. Davis. *The fundamental limit on binary switching energy for terascale integration (TSI)*. IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits 35(10), 1515-1516. 2000.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: E_min = kT ln2 for a binary transition (with error probability 1/2 at the limit, per contemporaneous press release quoting the authors); the 2 ln2 kT/q minimum supply of an ideal CMOS inverter is attributed to this line of work by secondary sources.
* Limits / supersession: Full text not inspected; the 2 ln2 kT/q value is carried as 'via secondary'.

**[kish2002]** L. B. Kish. *End of Moore's law: thermal (noise) death of integration in micro and nano electronics*. Physics Letters A 305, 144-149. 2002-12.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Thermal noise on shrinking node capacitances combined with falling supply voltage raises bit-error rates; motivates kT/C-based error estimates.

**[zhirnov2003]** V. V. Zhirnov, R. K. Cavin, J. A. Hutchby, G. I. Bourianoff. *Limits to binary logic switch scaling - a gedanken model*. Proceedings of the IEEE 91(11), 1934-1939. 2003.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Barrier-based binary switch model: distinguishability requires barrier E_b >~ kT ln(1/p); minimum-energy irreversible switch arguments.
* Limits / supersession: Contested by Lent and co-workers (adiabatic switching can dissipate < kT ln2); see 2007 comment/reply.

**[berut2012]** A. Berut, A. Arakelyan, A. Petrosyan, S. Ciliberto, R. Dillenschneider, E. Lutz. *Experimental verification of Landauer's principle linking information and thermodynamics*. Nature 483, 187-189. 2012-03-08. doi:10.1038/nature10872.

* Kind: measured data (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Colloidal particle in a modulated double-well potential; mean dissipated heat of erasure approaches kT ln2 in the limit of long (quasi-static) erasure cycles; excess heat grows as cycle time shortens.

**[jun2014]** Y. Jun, M. Gavrilov, J. Bechhoefer. *High-Precision Test of Landauer's Principle in a Feedback Trap*. Physical Review Letters 113, 190601. 2014. doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.190601.

* Kind: measured data (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Asymptotic erasure work 0.71 +/- 0.03 kT vs ln2 = 0.693 kT; dissipation in excess of kT ln2 scales ~1/tau with protocol duration tau.

**[si2019]** Bureau International des Poids et Mesures (BIPM). *The International System of Units (SI), 9th edition (2019)*. 2019-05-20. <https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure>

* Kind: standard. Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Exact defined values of k_B = 1.380649e-23 J/K, e = 1.602176634e-19 C, h = 6.62607015e-34 J s, N_A, c
* Limits / supersession: Constants are exact by definition; epsilon_0 is not (measured, CODATA).

**[proesmans2020]** K. Proesmans, J. Ehrich, J. Bechhoefer. *Finite-Time Landauer Principle*. Physical Review Letters 125, 100602. 2020-09-03. doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.125.100602. <https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2006.03242>

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Minimum average work to erase a bit in finite time exceeds kT ln2 by a term that grows as the protocol is shortened; closed form in the short-time limit.

**[freitas2021]** N. Freitas, J.-C. Delvenne, M. Esposito. *Stochastic Thermodynamics of Nonlinear Electronic Circuits: A Realistic Framework for Computing Around kT*. Physical Review X 11, 031064. 2021. doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.11.031064.

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Thermodynamically consistent noise model of MOS transistors in subthreshold; stochastic CMOS inverter deviates from deterministic behaviour near kT-scale energies.

**[gao_limmer2021]** C. Y. Gao, D. T. Limmer. *Principles of low dissipation computing from a stochastic circuit model*. 2021. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13067>

* Kind: theory / analysis (peer-reviewed). Access: bibliographic data and abstract-level content confirmed via a search service; full text not opened.
* Supports: Stochastic transistor-level model of NOT/NAND gates: high accuracy is achievable given enough energy and time, but at low energy accuracy and speed are coupled through device architecture.
